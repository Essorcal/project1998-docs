Tooling & fixes wave

2026-08-28 · Essorcal

GM & tester commands

  • A whole testing kit for walking the world: @clip no-clip (walls, mobs and players; per-session, always off at login; warps still work) — plus the client-side companion 5.33 testers run alongside it.
  • @anywarp — pass any gated doorway: level/mark/path gates, quest-locked warps, mythic zodiac caves, event caves, arena doors, path-hall class doors, even the quest-item tiles (Sute's seal, the lava row — nothing gets spent). Every gate still runs and echoes the denial it waived, so you can verify a gate while walking through it.
  • @showwarps — a blue pinwheel marker on every warp and scripted doorway of the map, visible only to you, following you across maps, with a printed list of destinations (quest-locked ones flagged). @showwarps look <frame> re-skins the markers live.
  • @quest / @legend — read and write the raw quest registry and legend marks directly (most chains gate on the legend, not the stage). Companion sheet: The Quest Registry.
  • @help finally paginates instead of scrolling off the pane, and @commands gives the compact index. The whole roster lives on The @Command Scroll.

Content, quests & world

  • The leviathan talisman can no longer be lost: using it doesn't eat it anymore (non-consumables survive u in general), and it refuses to be dropped, sold or traded anywhere but the pen — the bank still takes it.

Client & UI fixes

  • The examine tooltip follows the mouse now (the anchor was being read as a row index; it's a Y pixel).
  • 5.33 terrain: retracted four false "look-alike" object substitutions — the affected tiles draw their real art again.

Dev & map tools

  • Visual Map Editor — one-window app with the real 5.33 tile art: draft saves that never touch the shipped maps, floating minimap, marker layers for spawns/warps/NPCs with CSV row export, player-view toggle, walkable warps, new-map creation, and an opt-in direct mode that writes straight into game-data.
  • re: the map renderer decoded EPF frames and SObj records wrong — fixed, so rendered maps match the client.

Proximity broadcasts & fast-move

2026-08-20 · snugglezone

  • Proximity-limited broadcasts — combat, sound, and spell FX no longer go map-wide; sound uses RTK's tight SAMEAREA box (±9/±8, edge-shifted), damage bars and animations use the wider AREA box (±19/±17).
  • Fast-move persistence (the RE fix) — the client's runtime flag is driven verbatim from the 0x08 stats packet's body[46]; we were sending 0, silently forcing fast-move OFF on every stats refresh. Now driven from the saved preference, so it sticks when toggled and survives relog.

Multiplayer visibility & PvP

2026-08-18 · snugglezone

Multiplayer visibility & PvP

  • See players you walk up to — other players now stream in/out of view exactly like mobs (viewport-gated + tracked), fixing "can't see users I walk up to until I gate in next to them."
  • PvP melee — swinging at a player on a PvP map now deals real damage, sharing one live-validated damage formula between mob and player hits (target AC, positional rear-×2, Harden Body, deduction all applied).
  • Arena ghosts — a player killed on a PvP map becomes a hidden, no-clip ghost: invisible to the living (no corpse-camping), but visible to and blocking other ghosts.
  • Player collision — two players can no longer share a tile or no-clip through each other.

Cave ambush traps

  • Hidden ambush tiles in the mythic caves now burst mobs when stepped on and relocate a replacement trap (under a per-map mob cap), porting RTK's mob_spawn/rabbitTrap/tigerTrap. Warrior Watchful Eye reveals them; Rogue Spot Traps reveals the combat-trap family. Fall caves leave a cosmetic "shiver" echo.

Chat & social

  • Group/clan chat — whisper to !! for party chat and ! for clan chat.
  • Proximity speech — NPC bubbles, mob talk, and cast shouts now carry only to nearby players (say ±9/8, shout ±16) instead of map-wide.
  • Hand item / hand gold (h/H + gold gesture) now fold into the trade window, with a quest-turn-in hook for NPCs.
  • Group "Join a group" status stays in sync on form/join/leave/disband.

Items & spells

  • Mage weapon penalty — mages read a weapon's small-damage range at ~¼ value (live-measured), plus measured classFactor staircases for warrior/rogue/mage.
  • Confuse spell — a chance-based aggro reset that can set two adjacent mobs fighting each other; Amnesia gains a take-hold chance.
  • Rearrange panes (Shift+C) — swap bag slots and spellbook slots in place.

Options, battle cries & Nagnang

2026-08-17 · snugglezone

Options menu

  • Magic Effect, Weather Change, and Wisdom now actually do something — and all four synced options (those three plus Fast Move) now show the correct state, persist across closing/reopening the menu, and survive relog.
  • Magic Effect off now suppresses spell graphics and sounds for you.
  • Wisdom streams periodic gameplay hints into your chat channel (~every 15 min) when on.
  • Opening the Options menu (F10) no longer dismounts your horse.

Combat

  • Power-strike battle cries (Berserk's K'YA~!, Whirlwind's Sa-AAA~~!, Assault, Desperate Attack, Lethal Strike, etc.) now shout overhead instead of dumping into the chat box, and play the swing animation rather than the spell-cast pose.
  • Whirlwind and Berserk can now strike other players in PvP areas.
  • Casting mechanics reworked; melee re-measured; item wards now actually apply.

World & content

  • Nagnang added to the world map with its warp corridors opened.
  • New quests: the Dog Linguist and Old Dog dog-spell questlines; trainer secrets are now locked to their home city.
  • Spell rebalance: no-cast maps, buff slots, and post-cast costs.
  • Spawn system overhauled — per-creature respawn times and batched spawn groups.
  • Drinks now play a sip pose.

Social & misc

  • Group join/leave notices now go to the status pane instead of the chat box.
  • Character facing now persists across relog.
  • New GM and Tester command: @rez.
