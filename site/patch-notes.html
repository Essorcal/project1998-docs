Server: the Dog ignores a PC-subpath member's cleanse, the Chapel's divorce and engagement costs stop wrapping, Ki strike and Ice bombardment can be deflected, closing an NPC's page takes nothing, and two settings for operators

2026-10-26 · Essorcal · Server PRs #396, #397, #399, #400, #401, and, for operators, #382, #383

For players: the Dog's cleanse and PC subpaths (PR #401)

  • A PC-subpath member (Barbarian, Chongun, Do, Merchant, Ranger, Spy, Diviner, Geomancer, Shaman, Druid, Monk or Muse) who says "cleanse" to a Dog now gets no question and loses no karma, as in the server we port from. Their path, rank and spells stay as they were. Until now the Dog of their base class asked, took 4 karma and changed nothing.
  • @subpath leave is still how a PC-subpath member leaves.
  • Base classes and NPC subpaths (Chung ryong, Baekho, Ju jak, Hyun moo) at their own Dog: unchanged.

For players: the Chapel's costs for very large pools (PR #400)

  • A divorce costs 2,550 experience for each point of base vita. Above 1,684,300 base vita that sum used to wrap round and charge only a few hundred experience. It now asks the full amount, as in the server we port from. That is more experience than anyone can hold, so "Yes" leads to the usual sacrifice offer (8,000 vita or 4,000 mana).
  • Breaking an engagement costs 1,000 experience for each point of base mana. Above 4,294,967 base mana it used to wrap the same way; it now takes all banked experience, as in the server we port from.
  • Below those pools, nothing changes.

For testers: Ki strike and Ice bombardment can be deflected (PR #397)

  • PC-subpath spells reach testers only today (with @spell); members will learn them from their subpath's teacher once that lands.
  • Ki strike (Do) and Ice bombardment (Geomancer) can now be deflected, as in the server we port from: by a creature, through its protection and its Will over the caster's, as every other deflectable spell here; and by a player on a PvP map, through their Will over the caster's. A caster whose Will is at least the target's is never deflected by a player.
  • A deflected cast still costs its mana, as every deflected spell here does, and a deflected Ki strike still starts its 7-second wait. Nothing is struck, neither the target nor anything beside it. The caster reads "Your magic has been deflected." and then "You cast Ki strike." (or "Ice bombardment").
  • Beyond the spells' 10-tile reach the deflect roll now comes first. A creature 11 or more tiles away can answer "Your magic has been deflected." where it used to say "Ki strike cannot reach that far.". Both cost the same.
  • A cast that is not deflected is unchanged. Hailstorm, which works the same way, is still never deflected, as in the server we port from.

Checked: closing an NPC's page takes nothing

  • The 10-24 notes said closing an NPC's dialog page might count as going on to the next page, which could cost a player who backs out of Baek's offer. A read of the 4.95 game client settles it: closing a page (the close button, Esc, walking away) sends nothing to the server. Only the next and previous arrows, a menu pick and a typed answer reply. So closing Baek's offer, or any page that comes before a price, takes nothing, as in the server we port from.
  • Found in the same check, not fixed yet: after a page is closed that way, the server keeps waiting for an answer that never comes. Until the player changes map or finishes another NPC conversation, the event cave and Forever Tree entrances refuse the step, and the fox, the mage-stone spirit, the crow, the Watcher and the gauntlet altar don't appear. A fix is in review.

For operators: where new characters start, a synthetic test profile, and two earlier fixes (PRs #396, #399; #382, #383)

  • Start point: a new setting, P1998_START_POINT=map,x,y, puts every new character on that map and tile instead of the usual start. It is empty by default, and empty changes nothing. The login server checks it at start-up against map_index.csv and refuses to start on a value it cannot use (not three numbers, an unknown map, a tile off the map). It reads the map's size once, at start-up, so give both servers the same content folder. It is meant for test content sets that have no starting town of their own.
  • Synthetic profile: test-content/synthetic/ is a small invented content set for client testing: two maps, walls and objects of every kind, warps both ways and one standing NPC, with a manifest. It is generated by dotnet run --project Tools -- synthetic-profile and never used unless a server is pointed at it.
  • Earlier fixes: behind a trusted proxy, the game port's re-login limit and IP bans now use the player's own address (PR #382), and an oversized map request is clamped to one frame (PR #383). Players on a direct connection see no change from either.

Boards and the mailbox open on their newest 20 entries and load 20 older ones as you scroll (PR #395)

  • Opening a board or the mailbox, walking up to a board sign, and the list's refresh after a pop-up (after posting, for one) always start at the newest 20.
  • On the 4.95 client, a board or mailbox of 128 or more entries used to open empty or show only some of them (one of 300 showed 44). These lists now open normally.
  • Unchanged: a board or mailbox of 20 or fewer entries comes out exactly as before. No text is new or reworded.
  • Tests: a board and a mailbox of 300 entries are paged through to their first entry, each entry once, newest first, and those cases fail without the change; every list of 20 or fewer is checked to come out exactly as before.
  • Not checked: this has not been tried on a live client yet. The twelve PC subpaths can be joined, and only their members can use the subpath channel (PR #393)

  • Every Warrior, Rogue, Mage and Poet, and every Chung ryong, Baekho, Ju jak and Hyun moo, meets this: base classes and the NPC subpaths can no longer use the subpath channel (/sp), as in the server we port from. A line sent there now gets "You cannot do that." and nobody hears it. Until now every class could use the channel to talk to its own class.
  • The subpath channel now carries lines only between members of the same PC subpath; any other player is refused the same way. Whispering to the name "@" now reaches the channel too, as in the server we port from, under the same rule. F2 still turns subpath chat on and off for everyone, but for anyone outside the twelve PC subpaths it never carries a line.
  • The twelve PC subpaths can now be joined. A GM, or an elder the GM appoints for that subpath, admits a player of its base class at level 50 or higher: a Warrior to Barbarian, Chongun or Do; a Rogue to Merchant, Ranger or Spy; a Mage to Diviner, Geomancer or Shaman; a Poet to Druid, Monk or Muse. The player keeps their rank, takes the subpath's title, badge and chat label, can wear its gear, and can use its channel.
  • Admission is refused below level 50, to the wrong base class, to someone already in a subpath, and to anyone still holding the Dog's secrets, which no PC subpath may keep; the Dog must take them back first.
  • A member can leave, and a GM or the subpath's elder can expel a member. Either way the player goes back to their base class with their rank, and loses the subpath's own spells from their spellbook.
  • All of this goes through the new @subpath command, now in every player's @commands and @help list. On its own it shows your standing; @subpath leave leaves your subpath; GMs and elders admit and expel with it.
  • Members keep learning their base class's spells at its trainer (a Barbarian at the Warrior trainer), so a member admitted at 50 can still learn those from 51 to 99. They can also still enter their base class's guild hall.
  • Members fight in melee as their base class: a Barbarian hits like a Warrior of the same level in the same gear, a Diviner like a Mage (with the Mage's reduced weapon damage), and so on. Members of the NPC subpaths now swing as their base class too (a Chung ryong as a Warrior); before, every subpath swung with a Peasant's class bonus. The base classes' own swings do not change.
  • The NPC subpaths keep their trainer menu and guild-hall door as they were; for them only the channel and the melee swing change.
  • Not yet: nobody teaches a PC subpath's own spells. No trainer offers them; the subpath teachers come later. Testers can still teach them with @spell.
  • Not checked: whether, in 2001, a subpath member also had the Warrior's extra 2 damage with a weapon, or the Mage's reduced weapon damage, has not been measured; here both follow the base class.
  • Tests: new cases cover joining, leaving, the channel, F2, the trainer, the guild-hall door and the melee swing; breaking each part of the change turns one of them red.

Server: the first hit on a sleeping or dozing creature rounds down once, like every other hit

2026-10-23 · Essorcal · Server PR #378, and, as an internal fix, #371 (how the mentorship's lines reach the mentor; nothing players see changes)

The first hit on a sleeping or dozing creature rounds down once, like every other hit (PR #378, issue #363)

  • Every player who hits a creature put to sleep by Sleep or Doze meets this. The first hit that lands on such a creature takes its extra damage (1.5 times under Sleep, 1.3 under Doze) and uses it up. That hit now follows the one rule every other hit has followed since PR #361 (below): everything is multiplied together, rounded down once, and only then is a hit from behind doubled. Before, the extra damage was multiplied in after the round-down and the doubling, and rounded again. This fixes the first of the two items PR #361 listed as found, not fixed.
  • From in front, that first hit lands at most 1 HP away from what it landed before, up or down; from behind, at most 2 HP.
  • In a rare case it moves by 3 HP: a big swing (200 or more before armour) from behind a dozing creature, 11 of the 784,000 swings from behind a dozing creature that were checked. In each, the old figure was already 1 HP short before the extra damage came in, and the old order carried that to 3; the new figure is the exact one. For example, a swing of 200 at a creature of AC +15, from behind while it dozes, landed 595 and now lands 598.
  • A trap, a creature's poison tick, a pet's swing and a harvest swing at a sleeping or dozing creature now land the same or 1 HP lower: their extra damage is rounded down, where it used to be rounded to the nearest. On a creature that is not asleep or dozing, they land exactly what they did.
  • Unchanged: every hit on a creature that is not asleep or dozing, from in front and from behind; Backstab and Flank's half-damage reach, which is the second item PR #361 listed and is kept as it is; Sleep and Doze themselves, their durations and their lines; and hits between players.
  • Tests: real swings from in front and from behind, Spark and the Mage's Bolt, and the other kinds of blow, each at a sleeping and a dozing creature. Each case with extra damage fails without the change; the three without it pass before and after.
  • Found, not fixed: a Rogue's sleep trap holds the creature that springs it for 38 s, but that creature takes no extra damage, where in the server we port from a trapped creature takes extra damage, as a dozing one does. A poison tick on a sleeping or dozing creature uses up its extra damage, so the next hit lands without it; in the server we port from, a poison tick does not use it up. A poison tick never takes a creature's last HP on its own, but on a sleeping or dozing creature the extra damage is added after that limit, so a tick can still kill it. All three were so before this change. None is fixed yet.
